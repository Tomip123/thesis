## Article 28

## Contractual transparency obligations on international access and transfer

1. Providers of data processing services shall make the following information available on their websites, and keep that information up to date:
2. (a) the jurisdiction to which the ICT infrastructure deployed for data processing of their individual services is subject;
3. (b) a  general  description  of  the  technical,  organisational  and  contractual  measures  adopted  by  the  provider  of  data processing services in order  to prevent international governmental access to or  transfer of non-personal data held in the  Union where such access  or  transfer  would  create  a  conflict  with  Union  law or  the  national  law  of  the  relevant Member State.
2. The websites referred to in paragraph 1 shall be listed in contracts for all data processing services offered by providers of data processing services.

## Generated objectives (3)

### 1. Contractual Transparency Website Listing  _Article 28(2), 4 criteria_

**Description:** The contract lists the website(s) publishing the Article 28(1) international-access transparency information for the data processing service(s) it covers.

**Criteria:**
0. A reference to the specific website hosting the required jurisdiction and safeguards disclosures.
1. The website reference applies to the data processing service(s) governed by this contract.
2. The reference identifies international-access transparency information specifically, not a generic company or support page.
3. A commitment that the referenced website's transparency information is kept up to date.

### 2. ICT Infrastructure Jurisdiction Transparency  _Article 28(1)(a), 3 criteria_

**Description:** The documentation discloses the jurisdiction governing the ICT infrastructure used for each data processing service.

**Criteria:**
0. The jurisdiction (country or legal system) governing the ICT infrastructure is named.
1. Jurisdiction is disclosed per individual service rather than as one blanket statement covering all services.
2. The disclosed jurisdiction covers the infrastructure actually used for data processing, not merely the provider's corporate domicile.

### 3. Disclosure of Safeguards Against Foreign Governmental Interception  _Article 28(1)(b), 3 criteria_

**Description:** The documentation provides a general description of the technical, organisational, and contractual measures adopted to prevent international governmental access to or transfer of non-personal Union data where this would conflict with Union or Member State law.

**Criteria:**
0. Description of technical measures adopted to prevent unlawful international governmental access to or transfer of non-personal data.
1. Description of organisational measures adopted for the same purpose.
2. Description of contractual measures adopted for the same purpose.
