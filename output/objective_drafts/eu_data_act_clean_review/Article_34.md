## Article 34

## Interoperability for the purposes of in-parallel use of data processing services

1. The requirements laid down in Article 23, Article 24, Article 25(2), points (a)(ii), (a)(iv), (e) and (f) and Article 30(2) to (5) shall also apply mutatis mutandis to providers of data processing services to facilitate interoperability for the purposes of in-parallel use of data processing services.
2. Where a data processing service is being used in parallel with another data processing service, the providers of data processing services may impose data egress charges, but only for the purpose of passing on egress costs incurred, without exceeding such costs.

## Generated objectives (2)

### 1. In-Parallel Service Interoperability  _Article 34(1), 3 criteria_

**Description:** The contract confirms that the switching-related interoperability obligations under Articles 23, 24, 25(2) and 30(2) to (5) also apply when the data processing service is used in parallel with another provider's service, not only during switching.

**Criteria:**
0. Provisions confirm that customer-facing switching obligations under Articles 23, 24 and 25(2) also apply to support use of the service in parallel with another data processing service, not solely upon contract termination.
1. Provisions confirm that technical interface obligations — open interfaces and interoperability specifications under Article 30(2) to (5) — apply to enable concurrent operation with another provider's data processing service.
2. Contract terms address interoperability specifically for ongoing, simultaneous use of multiple data processing services, rather than limiting such terms to the switching or exit process alone.

### 2. In-Parallel Data Egress Cost Restrictions  _Article 34(2), 3 criteria_

**Description:** The contract contains writings regarding data egress fees when the service is used in parallel with another data processing service, limited to passing on the actual costs incurred.

**Criteria:**
0. Provisions confirm that data egress charges may be imposed only in connection with use of the service in parallel with another data processing service.
1. Provisions limit the purpose of such egress charges to passing on the egress costs actually incurred by the provider, excluding any other charge purpose such as a profit margin.
2. Provisions cap egress charges so that they do not exceed the actual costs incurred, regardless of the stated purpose of the charge.
