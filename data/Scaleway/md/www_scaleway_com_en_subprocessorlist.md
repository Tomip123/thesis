# List of sub-processors

Last reviewed: July 2025

To offer its services, Scaleway may use third party data processors. This page aims to inform all of our customers about the sub-processors likely to process their personal data as part of our services, in accordance with our [Data Processing Agreement (DPA)](https://www.scaleway.com/en/contracts/) and [our Privacy Policy](https://www.scaleway.com/en/privacy-policy/) .

All of our sub-processors are subject to a strict security control procedure as well as a data protection control procedure to ensure a level of compliance equivalent to that provided by Scaleway and required by data protection regulations.

## Authorisation to engage new sub-processors

By using Scaleway's services and agreeing to our DPA, Customer gives Scaleway general authorization to add, modify or remove sub-processors from this list.
