**Technical, organizational and contractual measures to prevent international governmental access or access conflicting with Union law or national law of member states**

UpCloud's data centers in the EU are operated by UpCloud Oy, the parent company of UpCloud group registered in Finland. The data held in the data centers is subject to the jurisdiction of the EU Member State in which the data center is located and thus governmental access to the data center facilities is determined by the laws of the relevant EU Member State.

UpCloud's platform is operated and managed from Finland and all customer account information (name, contact details, payment information, IP addresses, resources deployed, etc.) is held by UpCloud Oy in Finland. Thus such data is protected by the laws of Finland and the EU. UpCloud Oy does not disclose any data to any governmental authorities unless required by laws of Finland. UpCloud rejects any information requests received from foreign governmental authorities and directs the requester to contact relevant Finnish authorities for mutual legal assistance, in accordance with the Finnish law.

UpCloud has implemented comprehensive technical and organizational measures to protect the data it holds. UpCloud's information security management system is certified to comply with ISO 27001 standard. The measures adopted by UpCloud include access management and control, regular security audits and bug bounty program, continuous monitoring, incident response protocols, employee training programs, and strict data protection policies to ensure the highest standards of information security and privacy. When using external service providers, UpCloud selects only reliable and reputable vendors, and all vendors undergo security, privacy and compliance review.
